DATES AND TIMES

DATES:
DATE - format YYYY-MM-DD
DATETIME - format: YYYY-MM-DD HH:MI:SS
TIMESTAMP - format: YYYY-MM-DD HH:MI:SS (in seconds)
TIME - format: HH:MI:SS
YEAR - format YYYY or YY

when entering the values of dates make sure you have used single inverted comma (' ')

values when inserting:
CURRENT_DATE() (date), CURRENT_TIME() (time), NOW() (datetime)
